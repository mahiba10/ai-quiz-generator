Lean Canvas — AI-Powered Quiz Generator and Personalized Learning Assistant

SDG 4: Quality Education. Items marked (assumption) are not yet validated.

1. Problem

  • Students cannot easily tell which topics they understand (assumption)
  • Practice feedback is often delayed or generic
  • Revision is unfocused

2. Existing Alternatives

  • Textbook end-of-chapter questions
  • Online quiz sites and YouTube practice videos
  • Classroom tests (infrequent, slow feedback)

3. Solution

  • Topic/difficulty-based MCQ practice with instant, explained feedback
  • Concept-level revision suggestions from actual answers
  • ML topic finder that suggests a quiz from a learning query

4. Key Metrics

  • Quizzes completed, average score change across attempts
  • Share of users who reattempt a weak concept
  • Topic-classifier accuracy/F1 (measured, small test set) (Targets to be defined after real usage; none measured yet.)

5. Unique Value Proposition

Free, offline-capable practice that tells you what to revise next, using verified questions.

6. High-Level Concept

"A self-check coach for college students: quiz, explain, recommend."

7. Unfair Advantage

None claimed at this stage. Possible future: a curated, syllabus-aligned question bank.

8. Channels

  • College clubs and class groups (assumption)
  • GitHub and Streamlit Community Cloud link
  • Social media posts by the author

9. Customer Segments

  • Undergraduate engineering students
  • Self-learners preparing for fundamentals in CS

10. Early Adopters

Classmates and juniors in the author's department (assumption; not yet tested).

11. Cost Structure

  • Free hosting (Streamlit Community Cloud) and open-source libraries
  • Author's time for writing and verifying questions

12. Revenue Streams

None at present (academic project). Possible future: institution licences or premium question packs (assumption, unvalidated).
